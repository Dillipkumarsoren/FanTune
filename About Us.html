About FanTune

FanTune is a simple and user-friendly offline media player designed for Android devices.

1. Who We Are

FanTune is created with the goal of providing users with a simple, convenient, and enjoyable way to play their personal audio and video files directly from their Android devices.

We believe that playing your own media should be simple, fast, and easy to understand.

2. What FanTune Does

FanTune is primarily designed to work with media files stored locally on your device.

Depending on the supported features of the application, FanTune may allow you to:

🎵 Audio Playback

Browse and play supported music and audio files stored on your device.

🎬 Video Playback

Play supported video files stored locally on your Android device.

📁 Local Media

Find and organize supported media available on your device for convenient playback.

⚡ Simple Experience

FanTune focuses on a clean and straightforward interface so users can quickly access their media.

3. Our Goal

Our goal is to make FanTune a lightweight and easy-to-use media player that provides a smooth playback experience without unnecessary complexity.

We continuously work to improve the application, fix bugs, improve compatibility, and introduce useful features.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or permissions to use the media they access through FanTune.
