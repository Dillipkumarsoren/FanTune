1. Introduction

FanTune ("we", "us", "our", or "FanTune") is an offline media player application designed to allow users to browse and play supported audio and video files available on their device.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information FanTune may access, how that information is used, and how we protect your privacy.

By using FanTune, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Access

2.1 Device Media Files

FanTune may request permission to access audio, video, images, or other media files stored on your device.

This access may be required to:

  • Find media files stored on your device.
  • Display available media in the application.
  • Show file names, thumbnails, duration, or artwork.
  • Play audio and video files.

FanTune does not intentionally upload your personal media files to our servers. Your media files remain on your device unless you explicitly share them using your device or another application.

2.2 Device Information

FanTune may process limited technical information required for the application to function properly, such as device type, Android version, application version, and basic diagnostic information.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless specifically disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by FanTune may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain application features.
  • Display media stored on your device.
  • Play audio and video files.
  • Improve application stability and performance.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Prevent misuse or abuse of the application.
  • Display advertisements, where applicable.

FanTune does not sell your personal information.

Third-party advertising providers may collect or process certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, or information related to advertising interactions, according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

FanTune may request permissions that are necessary for its functionality.

Depending on your Android version and the features available in FanTune, these permissions may include access to media files stored on your device.

You can manage or revoke permissions at any time through your Android device settings. Revoking required permissions may prevent some FanTune features from working correctly.

FanTune is not specifically designed for children under the age required by applicable laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through FanTune.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

FanTune does not maintain a personal account system unless explicitly stated in a future version of the application.

You can remove local media files through your device's normal file-management functions.

If third-party services used by FanTune process information, their respective retention and deletion policies may apply.

10. Sharing of Information
